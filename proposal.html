Overview

This proposal outlines the terms for ongoing access, support, and maintenance of LogiTrack — a custom-built owner dashboard designed specifically for independent transport and freight operators in South Africa. LogiTrack gives you full visibility into your trips, payouts, and expenses in one place, so you always know exactly where your business stands.

What's Included
🚚
Trip Management Log, edit, and track Delivery, IBT, and MrT trips with automatic payout calculation.
📊
Dashboard Analytics Live charts for income vs expenses, trip type breakdown, and 3-month cash flow forecast.
💰
Smart Rate Card 7 weight bands × 12 distance bands with long-distance override and 15% VAT handling.
🧾
Expense Tracking Categorised expense logging (Fuel, Repairs, Breakages, Other) with KPI summaries.
📥
Excel Export Export trips and expenses to formatted Excel files at any time.
🔒
Secure Access Email/password authentication via Supabase — your data stays private.
📱
Mobile Responsive Works on phone, tablet, and desktop — no app install needed.
⚙️
Rate Card Updates Any rate card changes you need during the contract period are included at no extra cost.
